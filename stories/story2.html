Chapter 1: The Late Night Passenger

Sarah checked her watch for the third time in as many minutes. 11:47 PM. The last train of the night was running late, and the deserted platform at Central Station felt more like a scene from a horror movie than her usual commute home.

The fluorescent lights flickered overhead, casting long shadows that seemed to move of their own accord. Sarah pulled her coat tighter around her shoulders, trying to ignore the growing sense of unease that had been building since she'd decided to work late at the office.

When the train finally arrived at 11:52 PM, it was nearly empty. Sarah chose a seat near the middle of the car, away from the few other passengers who seemed to be avoiding each other's gaze. She settled in with her book, determined to make the most of the quiet ride home.

But as the train pulled away from the station, Sarah noticed something strange. The other passengers—there were four of them—all seemed to be reading the same book. Not just similar books, but identical copies of what appeared to be a leather-bound volume with no visible title.

More unsettling was the fact that they were all turning pages in perfect synchronization, as if they were part of some bizarre reading ritual. Sarah tried to focus on her own book, but she couldn't shake the feeling that she was being watched.

At the next stop, a new passenger boarded. He was tall and thin, wearing a long black coat that seemed to absorb the light around him. When he sat down across from Sarah, she noticed that he wasn't carrying any luggage, not even a book like the others.

Instead, he simply stared out the window at the passing darkness, his reflection in the glass showing a face that seemed to shift and change with each passing streetlight. Sarah realized with growing horror that the reflection wasn't matching his movements.

The train continued through the night, and Sarah understood that this was no ordinary journey. She was trapped on the Midnight Express, and the destination was somewhere between reality and nightmare.
